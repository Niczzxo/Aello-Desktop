AELLO DESKTOP CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 0.1.0-beta4
DATE: 2026-10-28
STATUS: PRE-RELEASE

ABOUT
Aello Desktop is a privacy-first YouTube and YouTube Music client - no account, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry. It is the desktop companion to Aello for Android.

PROFILES
- Up to five profiles on one computer, each with its own recommendations, history, library and settings, so a study profile and a gaming profile never mix. Downloads are shared.
- Switch from your avatar in the top bar or in Settings > Profiles. Switching restarts Aello, and the new profile opens in its own theme.
- A new profile starts fresh with a short setup, or copies your settings, subscriptions or recommendations. Profiles are not locked: anyone using the computer can switch.
- Only the Default profile syncs with your phone. A master backup now holds every profile and restores them all.
- The recommendation engine's profile is now called your taste profile.

SYNC
- Your recommendations sync with Aello for Android again. Both apps now read Chinese titles the same way, folding Traditional characters into Simplified ones.
- When something stays on each device, the sync result now says what and why. After receiving, it lists what arrived.
- SponsorBlock's action for each segment type now syncs with your phone. Synced SponsorBlock and DeArrow switches apply without a restart.
- Data synced before comes back if this computer lost it. Music playlists from your phone no longer return under a new name, history sent to the phone has thumbnails, and subscription groups are sent when Aello Desktop scans the phone's code.
- Play on: moving playback between Aello Desktop and your phone works again. A phone that paused or stopped no longer stays listed as playing, and a phone at full volume no longer shows its volume at 0.
- Play on: picking a device now works as on Android. This computer takes playback back from the device you control, picking another device moves that device's session there, and a device that stops answering stops showing as playing.

FIXES
- Restarting Aello after an update no longer opens the link Aello was first started with a second time.
- A restarted AppImage starts from your own environment instead of the old bundle's paths.
- Aello opens in your theme from the first frame, instead of showing the default colours for a moment.
